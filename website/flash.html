Let's get your board online

Keep this page open and your ESP32 plugged in with a USB data cable. We will do the rest, one step at a time.

1 Flash the board 2 Connect to Wi-Fi 3 Copy the address
1

Flash the board

One click installs the ArduBuddy firmware v1.0 on your ESP32. The firmware is already included on this page.

Loading firmware information…

Not started yet
Board not connected
When it finishes: press the RESET (EN) button on the board, wait two seconds, then go to step 2. Don't unplug the cable.
Advanced: use my own firmware file or see the log
2

Connect to Wi-Fi

Type your Wi-Fi name and password. They go straight to the board through the USB cable — this page never uploads or saves them.

Not connected yet

We will connect the USB serial port, send your Wi-Fi details, then switch on device control.

Use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. ESP32 cannot join 5 GHz. If nothing happens, press RESET on the board and try again. A USB driver for CP210x or CH340 may be needed.
Advanced: fix connection problems and see the log
Advanced: the commands we send (no AI key involved)
Save Wi-Fi{"c":"ws","s":"Office-WiFi","p":"password"}
Check status{"c":"wst"}
Switch on the local API{"c":"start"}
Forget Wi-Fi{"c":"wr"}
Restart{"c":"rst"}

The ArduBuddy firmware v1.0 receives JSON commands over USB serial and HTTP. No AI model or API key runs on the board. You still need a WorkBuddy account.
